# 🤖 Anthropic Provider

## 📖 Overview

Anthropic is an AI safety and research company best known for its **Claude family of Large Language Models (LLMs)**. Claude models are optimized for **reasoning, software engineering, long-context understanding, enterprise AI, and agentic workflows** while emphasizing AI safety through Constitutional AI.

Anthropic provides models through its native Claude API and via major cloud platforms including Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. Current model families include **Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku**, and the limited-availability **Mythos** tier. ([Claude Platform][1])

## 📌 Notes

* **Fable 5** is Anthropic's flagship generally available model for demanding reasoning and long-running autonomous tasks.
* **Opus 5** targets complex enterprise coding and agentic workflows with a lower cost than Fable while maintaining high capability.
* **Sonnet 5** is the recommended balance of speed, intelligence, and cost for most production applications.
* **Haiku 4.5** is optimized for high-throughput, latency-sensitive workloads such as customer support and routing.
* **Mythos 5** is an invitation-only model intended for select enterprise and research customers. ([Claude Platform][1])
