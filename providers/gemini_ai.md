# 🤖 Google DeepMind Provider

## 📖 Overview

**Google DeepMind** is Google's AI research and product division, formed by combining **Google Brain** and **DeepMind**. It develops the **Gemini family of multimodal foundation models**, along with specialized models for image generation, video generation, speech, embeddings, robotics, scientific research, and enterprise AI.

Google's AI ecosystem spans **Gemini API**, **Vertex AI**, **AI Studio**, **Workspace AI**, **Android**, **Search**, **Cloud AI**, and **Google Agentspace**, making it one of the most comprehensive AI platforms available.

## 📌 Notes

* **Gemini 3 Pro** is Google's flagship general-purpose multimodal model for enterprise AI, coding, and agentic workflows.
* **Gemini Deep Think** is optimized for complex reasoning and research-oriented tasks.
* **Gemini 3 Flash** balances capability, latency, and cost for most production deployments.
* **Gemini 3 Flash Lite** is designed for high-throughput, cost-sensitive workloads.
* **Imagen 4**, **Veo 3**, **Chirp 3**, and **Lyria 2** complement Gemini by providing specialized image, video, speech, and music generation capabilities.
* **Vertex AI** remains Google's primary enterprise platform for deploying, evaluating, governing, and monitoring AI applications at scale.
