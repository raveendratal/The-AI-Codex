# 🤖 OpenAI Provider

## 📖 Overview

OpenAI is one of the leading providers of frontier Large Language Models (LLMs), offering a comprehensive portfolio spanning **reasoning models, multimodal GPT models, lightweight edge models, embedding models, speech models, image generation, and content moderation**.

OpenAI models power enterprise copilots, AI agents, software engineering assistants, RAG systems, multimodal applications, autonomous workflows, and large-scale SaaS platforms.
