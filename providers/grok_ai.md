# 🤖 xAI Provider (Grok)

## 📖 Overview

**xAI** is an artificial intelligence company founded by **Elon Musk** with the mission of accelerating scientific discovery through AI. Its flagship model family, **Grok**, is designed for **advanced reasoning, software engineering, multimodal understanding, autonomous AI agents, and real-time knowledge retrieval**.

A key differentiator of Grok is its **native integration with X (formerly Twitter)**, enabling access to real-time public information and trending discussions. xAI also develops the **Colossus AI supercomputer**, one of the world's largest AI training infrastructures.

## 📌 Notes

* **Grok 4 Heavy** is xAI's highest-capability reasoning model, designed for complex research, scientific analysis, and long-running agentic workflows.
* **Grok 4** is the flagship balanced model for enterprise AI, software engineering, and multimodal applications.
* **Grok 4 Fast** is optimized for production deployments requiring lower latency and higher throughput.
* **Grok Mini** targets lightweight, cost-efficient conversational AI.
* **Aurora** complements the Grok family with high-quality text-to-image generation and image editing.
* Grok's integration with **live web search** and the **X platform** makes it particularly well suited for applications that depend on current events, social media trends, and continuously changing information.
