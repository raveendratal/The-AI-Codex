# 🤖 Z.AI Provider (GLM Family)

## 📖 Overview

**Z.AI** (formerly associated with the GLM ecosystem from Zhipu AI) is an enterprise AI platform focused on **Agentic AI, software engineering, reasoning, multimodal AI, and open-weight foundation models**. Its flagship **GLM-5** series is designed for long-horizon agent execution, coding, enterprise assistants, and multimodal applications. The platform also provides specialized models for vision, OCR, embeddings, image generation, speech, and presentation generation. ([Overview - Z.AI DEVELOPER DOCUMENT][1])

## 📌 Notes

* **GLM-5.1** extends the GLM family with stronger support for **long-horizon autonomous tasks**, aiming to execute multi-step workflows over extended periods. ([Overview - Z.AI DEVELOPER DOCUMENT][5])
* **GLM-5** is the flagship foundation model for enterprise reasoning, coding, and agentic engineering, featuring a **200K-token context window**. ([Overview - Z.AI DEVELOPER DOCUMENT][2])
* **GLM-5V-Turbo** combines multimodal understanding with coding and visual programming capabilities. ([Overview - Z.AI DEVELOPER DOCUMENT][5])
* **GLM-Image** is optimized for generating complex visual assets such as technical posters, presentations, and educational diagrams. ([Overview - Z.AI DEVELOPER DOCUMENT][3])
* Z.AI's roadmap emphasizes **agentic AI**, **software engineering**, and **enterprise automation**, making the GLM family particularly attractive for organizations building autonomous AI systems. ([Overview - Z.AI DEVELOPER DOCUMENT][2])
